# Issue 7 — Separate viewport impact from total-page work

## Problem

3D models and background assets may extend total page completion without delaying
LCP or initial viewport rendering. A total-page signal is currently easy to read
as an LCP cause.

## Required change

Classify findings by impact surface: initial viewport, LCP/FCP, interaction, or
total page completion. Require timing overlap before connecting background work
to a CWV.

## Acceptance

An asset loaded after LCP can be reported as total-page work but cannot become an
LCP root cause without overlap evidence.
